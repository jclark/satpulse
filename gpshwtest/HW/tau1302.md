# TAU1302 limitations

How device-independent configuration is realized on the Allystar
TAU1302 (HD9310), relative to perfect realization of the full model
(`SEMANTICS.md`). Measured on firmware 3.018.f3667a12 (2026-07-03,
UART at 115200). Perfectly-realized behavior is not listed.

## Signals

Supported signal set (L1/L2 dual band):

| GNSS | Signals |
|------|---------|
| GPS  | L1 C/A, L2C |
| GAL  | E1 |
| GLO  | L1, L2 |
| BDS  | B1I, B2I |
| QZSS | L1 C/A, L2C |

- This unit's silicon COUPLES: writing a Galileo E5 bit it lacks
  switches Galileo E1 on (found by gpshwtest's syntax-equivalence
  check). satpulse therefore intersects requests with the
  identity-deduced plan before writing, so unsupported bits never
  reach the wire on known hardware; the coupling remains latent for
  unknown models, where the verify readback reports whatever the
  silicon did.
- The unit ships with a selection narrower than its capability:
  GLONASS and QZSS are disabled in its as-found mask.

## Properties

Same shape as the other two units: no antenna cable delay (CFG-PPS
Offset is factory calibration, 0 ns on this unit); ECEF-only fixed
position storage at 0.01 m quantum with no stored accuracy; float32
minimum elevation; no pulse alignment or timing constellation; the
active UART is not identifiable.

## Message output

- `tp`, `leap` (announcement date), and per-signal `sig` deliver
  nothing.
- RTCM MSM4 and MSM7 emit per enabled constellation. Unlike the
  TAU951M, this unit emits a 1005 ARP even with a zero fixed position.
- The unit SHIPS with RTCM broadcast ephemerides (1019/1042/1046 for
  its enabled constellations) and MSM7 enabled and emitting. The
  ephemeris types are outside the `--rtcm-out` group vocabulary and
  are left untouched by RTCM requests, like TXT in the NMEA group, so
  the shipped behavior survives configuration.
- Raw output is the single RXM-DUMPRAW switch (~2.3 KB frames).

## Testing notes

As-found running configuration: NMEA GGA, GSA, GSV, RMC, ZDA at 1 Hz
(TXT off); mobile mode; GPS L1+L2C, BDS B1I+B2I, GAL E1 selected
(GLONASS/QZSS off); RTCM ephemerides + MSM7 at rate 1; CFG-CFG load
(action 1) actually works in place on this unit, unlike the other two
where it is acknowledged and ignored - reload is realized as a soft
reset everywhere regardless. A full non-disruptive run takes about
20 minutes and leaves the receiver as found.
