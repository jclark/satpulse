## Specialist vendors

* [ArduSimple](https://www.ardusimple.com/) are in Andorra and Spain, and are primarily focuses on the RTK case. They make an M.2 card with antenna and time pulse out connectors that can fit in a wifi M.2 slot in a PC.
* [Beitian](https://www.beitian.com/en/ are a Chinese company that make a range of GNSS boards, modules and antennas. They have their own direct [store](https://store.beitian.com/), but I have bought through AliExpress.
* [BG7TBL](https://bg7tbl.taobao.com/) is a rather mysterious Chinese vendor. The nearest to an official website is their Taobao store. They make some nice external GNSS units. They also make an antenna splitter.
* [FS](https://www.fs.com/) make some inexpensive switches with PTP support.
* [gnss.store](https://gnss.store/) are in Latvia. They make some USB dongle with SMA connectors for antenna and time pulse out.
* [Leo Bodnar Electronics][https://www.leobodnar.com/shop/] are a UK company, which makes some GNSS and timing products.
* [SparkFun Electronics](https://www.sparkfun.com/) make a variety of GNSS boards. I haven't used them much because their US location is not convenient for me.
* [Star River](http://www.sragps.com/) is a Chinese company that make a number of GNSS boards that are inexpensive and work well in a CM4/5.
They have stores on [AliExpress](https://xinghewei.aliexpress.com) and on [Taobao](https://sragps.taobao.com). The Taobao shop seems to have a wider range.
* [Time Appliances](https://www.tindie.com/stores/timeappliances/) make the TimeHAT, which is a hat for a Raspberry Pi 5, with an Intel i226 NIC.
* [TimeBeat](https://store.timebeat.app/) are in London, UK. They make a board for the CM4/5 in a unique "sandwich" form factor. 


## Distributors

* [AliExpress](http://www.aliexpress.com) is a China-based marketplace intended for buyers outside China. It is important to understand that AliExpress does not sell products itself. It is a platform that connects buyers and sellers. The key thing is to choose the seller the carefully. They have ratings out of 5, but it's best to ignore the leading 4 and treat it as a rating out of 10, i.e. consider a seller with a rating of 4.5 as having a rating of 5/10. I have bought a lot from AliExpress, and my experiences have overall been very good. Shipping times can be long.
* [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/) is a large US-based electronics distributor. They are a good source for u-blox EVKs.
* [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/) have a lot of resellers of Chinese-made products. It can be a little less daunting for a Western buyer than AliExpress or Taobao, although the prices can be higher.
* [Mouser Electronic](https://www.mouser.com/) is another large US-based electronics distributor. I have had slightly better experience with Digi-Key.
* [Taobao](https://www.taobao.com/) is a China-based marketplace. It is primarily designed for residents of China, but they can ship direct to some countries outside China in the Asia-Pacific region. If you don't live in one of these countries, it's a bit of a challenge to use (Google translate helps a lot), but it is possible. I have found the vendors to be generally honest. As with AliExpress, you need to carefully choose which stores you buy from. Shipping times tend to be long.
* [Tindie](https://www.tindie.com/) is a US-based market place for small electronics vendors.
